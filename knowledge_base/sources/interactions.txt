DRUG INTERACTIONS AND CONTRAINDICATIONS

CRITICAL INTERACTIONS (Avoid or use with extreme caution):

Warfarin + Aspirin
- Severity: CRITICAL
- Risk: Significantly increased bleeding risk
- Mechanism: Both drugs affect platelet function and coagulation
- Recommendation: Avoid combination unless absolutely necessary. If used, monitor INR closely.

Warfarin + Ibuprofen
- Severity: CRITICAL
- Risk: Increased bleeding risk and GI ulceration
- Mechanism: Ibuprofen inhibits platelet aggregation and can cause GI bleeding
- Recommendation: Avoid combination. Use paracetamol for pain relief instead.

Warfarin + Metformin
- Severity: HIGH
- Risk: Increased risk of bleeding complications
- Mechanism: Metformin may affect coagulation parameters
- Recommendation: Monitor INR more frequently if combination is necessary.

Aspirin + Ibuprofen
- Severity: HIGH
- Risk: Increased GI bleeding and ulceration
- Mechanism: Both are NSAIDs with similar side effects
- Recommendation: Avoid combination. Use one or the other, not both.

Metformin + Furosemide
- Severity: HIGH
- Risk: Increased risk of lactic acidosis
- Mechanism: Both can cause dehydration and electrolyte imbalance
- Recommendation: Monitor electrolytes and renal function closely.

HIGH RISK INTERACTIONS:

ACE Inhibitors (Lisinopril) + Potassium-sparing diuretics
- Severity: HIGH
- Risk: Hyperkalemia
- Mechanism: Both increase serum potassium
- Recommendation: Monitor potassium levels regularly.

Statins (Atorvastatin, Simvastatin) + Fibrates
- Severity: HIGH
- Risk: Increased risk of rhabdomyolysis
- Mechanism: Both can cause muscle damage
- Recommendation: Monitor CK levels and muscle symptoms.

Beta-blockers (Metoprolol) + Calcium channel blockers (Amlodipine)
- Severity: MODERATE
- Risk: Excessive bradycardia and hypotension
- Mechanism: Both lower heart rate and blood pressure
- Recommendation: Monitor heart rate and blood pressure closely.

MODERATE INTERACTIONS:

SSRIs (Sertraline) + NSAIDs
- Severity: MODERATE
- Risk: Increased bleeding risk
- Mechanism: SSRIs affect platelet function
- Recommendation: Monitor for signs of bleeding.

Proton pump inhibitors (Omeprazole) + Clopidogrel
- Severity: MODERATE
- Risk: Reduced effectiveness of clopidogrel
- Mechanism: Omeprazole may inhibit clopidogrel activation
- Recommendation: Consider alternative PPI or H2 blocker.

GENERAL PRINCIPLES:
- Always check for drug interactions before prescribing multiple medications
- Use drug interaction databases for comprehensive checking
- Monitor patients closely when using interacting medications
- Consider alternative medications when possible
- Educate patients about signs and symptoms of adverse effects
