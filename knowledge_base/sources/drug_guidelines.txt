DRUG DOSAGE GUIDELINES

Aspirin (Acetylsalicylic Acid)
- Standard dose: 75-100 mg once daily for cardiovascular protection
- Maximum dose: 4000 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >4000 mg/day can cause gastrointestinal bleeding
- Pediatric: Not recommended under 16 years

Ibuprofen
- Standard dose: 200-400 mg every 4-6 hours
- Maximum dose: 3200 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >3200 mg/day increases risk of GI ulcers and renal impairment
- Duration: Should not exceed 10 days without medical supervision

Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
- Standard dose: 500-1000 mg every 4-6 hours
- Maximum dose: 4000 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >4000 mg/day can cause severe liver damage
- Critical: Single dose >7500 mg requires immediate medical attention

Metformin
- Standard dose: 500-1000 mg twice daily
- Maximum dose: 2550 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >2550 mg/day increases risk of lactic acidosis
- Monitoring: Check creatinine and eGFR before and during treatment

Warfarin
- Standard dose: 2-10 mg once daily (dose varies based on INR)
- Maximum dose: 10 mg per day (rarely exceeded)
- Overdose risk: >10 mg/day significantly increases bleeding risk
- Monitoring: INR must be checked regularly (target 2-3 for most conditions)

Amoxicillin
- Standard dose: 250-500 mg three times daily
- Maximum dose: 3000 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >3000 mg/day can cause severe diarrhea and Clostridium difficile infection
- Duration: Typically 7-14 days for bacterial infections

Atorvastatin
- Standard dose: 10-20 mg once daily
- Maximum dose: 80 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >80 mg/day increases risk of rhabdomyolysis
- Monitoring: Check liver function tests (ALT, AST) and CK before and during treatment

Simvastatin
- Standard dose: 10-20 mg once daily
- Maximum dose: 80 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >80 mg/day increases risk of muscle damage
- Monitoring: Liver function tests and CK required

Omeprazole
- Standard dose: 20-40 mg once daily
- Maximum dose: 80 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >80 mg/day may cause hypomagnesemia
- Duration: Long-term use (>1 year) requires monitoring

Lisinopril
- Standard dose: 10-20 mg once daily
- Maximum dose: 40 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >40 mg/day increases risk of hyperkalemia and renal impairment
- Monitoring: Check potassium and creatinine

Amlodipine
- Standard dose: 5-10 mg once daily
- Maximum dose: 10 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >10 mg/day can cause severe hypotension
- Monitoring: Blood pressure monitoring required

Metoprolol
- Standard dose: 50-100 mg twice daily
- Maximum dose: 200 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >200 mg/day can cause bradycardia and heart block
- Monitoring: Heart rate and blood pressure

Furosemide
- Standard dose: 20-80 mg once or twice daily
- Maximum dose: 600 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >600 mg/day can cause severe dehydration and electrolyte imbalance
- Monitoring: Electrolytes (Na, K, Cl) and creatinine required

Levothyroxine
- Standard dose: 25-150 mcg once daily
- Maximum dose: 300 mcg per day
- Overdose risk: >300 mcg/day can cause hyperthyroidism
- Monitoring: TSH levels required

Gabapentin
- Standard dose: 300-600 mg three times daily
- Maximum dose: 3600 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >3600 mg/day increases risk of sedation and respiratory depression
- Monitoring: Renal function (dose adjustment needed in renal impairment)

Sertraline
- Standard dose: 50-100 mg once daily
- Maximum dose: 200 mg per day
- Overdose risk: >200 mg/day increases risk of serotonin syndrome
- Monitoring: Monitor for mood changes and suicidal ideation
