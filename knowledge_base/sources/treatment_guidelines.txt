TREATMENT GUIDELINES - WHO, NHS, AND CLINICAL PROTOCOLS

DIABETES MELLITUS TYPE 2
- First-line: Metformin 500-1000 mg twice daily
- Second-line: Add sulfonylurea, DPP-4 inhibitor, or GLP-1 agonist
- Target HbA1c: <7.0% for most patients
- Monitoring: HbA1c every 3-6 months, annual eye exam, foot exam
- Lifestyle: Diet, exercise, weight management
- WHO Guidelines: Metformin is first-line unless contraindicated

HYPERTENSION
- First-line: ACE inhibitor (Lisinopril) or ARB, or Calcium channel blocker (Amlodipine)
- Target: <140/90 mmHg for most, <130/80 for high-risk patients
- Combination: Often requires 2-3 medications
- Monitoring: Blood pressure every 3-6 months
- Lifestyle: Low sodium diet, exercise, weight loss
- WHO Guidelines: Treat if BP >140/90 mmHg

HYPERLIPIDEMIA
- First-line: Statin (Atorvastatin 10-20 mg or Simvastatin 20-40 mg)
- Target LDL: <100 mg/dL for high risk, <70 mg/dL for very high risk
- Monitoring: Lipid panel 4-12 weeks after starting, then annually
- Lifestyle: Low-fat diet, exercise
- WHO Guidelines: Statin therapy for primary and secondary prevention

HYPOTHYROIDISM
- Treatment: Levothyroxine 25-150 mcg once daily
- Target TSH: 0.4-4.0 mIU/L (individualized)
- Monitoring: TSH 6-8 weeks after starting or dose change
- Duration: Lifelong treatment typically required
- WHO Guidelines: Treat with levothyroxine to normalize TSH

BACTERIAL INFECTIONS
- First-line: Amoxicillin 250-500 mg three times daily for 7-14 days
- Alternative: Consider local resistance patterns
- Monitoring: Clinical response, consider culture if no improvement
- Duration: Complete full course even if symptoms improve
- WHO Guidelines: Use antibiotics judiciously to prevent resistance

PAIN MANAGEMENT
- Mild pain: Paracetamol 500-1000 mg every 4-6 hours
- Moderate pain: Ibuprofen 200-400 mg every 4-6 hours (max 3200 mg/day)
- Severe pain: Consider opioids (with caution)
- Duration: Limit NSAIDs to 10 days unless supervised
- WHO Guidelines: Stepwise approach to pain management

GASTROESOPHAGEAL REFLUX DISEASE (GERD)
- First-line: Lifestyle modifications (diet, weight loss, elevation of head)
- Second-line: Proton pump inhibitor (Omeprazole 20-40 mg daily)
- Duration: 4-8 weeks initially, then as needed
- Monitoring: Consider endoscopy if persistent symptoms
- WHO Guidelines: PPI for moderate-severe GERD

DEPRESSION
- First-line: SSRI (Sertraline 50-100 mg daily)
- Target: Improvement in symptoms within 4-6 weeks
- Duration: Continue for 6-12 months after remission
- Monitoring: Assess for suicidal ideation, especially in first few weeks
- WHO Guidelines: SSRI as first-line treatment for moderate-severe depression

ATRIAL FIBRILLATION (Anticoagulation)
- Treatment: Warfarin (target INR 2.0-3.0) or DOAC
- Indication: CHA2DS2-VASc score ≥2 for men, ≥3 for women
- Monitoring: INR every 1-4 weeks for warfarin
- Duration: Long-term unless contraindicated
- WHO Guidelines: Anticoagulation for stroke prevention in AF

HEART FAILURE
- Treatment: ACE inhibitor + Beta-blocker + Diuretic (Furosemide)
- Target: Symptom control, prevent hospitalizations
- Monitoring: Weight, symptoms, electrolytes, renal function
- Lifestyle: Low sodium diet, fluid restriction if indicated
- WHO Guidelines: Combination therapy for heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

GENERAL PRINCIPLES:
- Follow evidence-based guidelines (WHO, NHS, professional societies)
- Individualize treatment based on patient factors
- Monitor for efficacy and adverse effects
- Adjust treatment based on response
- Consider drug interactions and contraindications
- Educate patients about their medications
- Regular follow-up and monitoring essential
