MEDICAL RED FLAGS - EMERGENCY SYMPTOMS REQUIRING IMMEDIATE ATTENTION

CRITICAL RED FLAGS:

Chest Pain
- Description: Any chest pain, especially if severe, crushing, or radiating
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Rule out myocardial infarction
- Action: ECG, cardiac enzymes, immediate medical evaluation
- Associated symptoms: Shortness of breath, nausea, sweating, arm/jaw pain

Shortness of Breath
- Description: Difficulty breathing, especially if sudden onset
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Could indicate pulmonary embolism, heart failure, or asthma
- Action: Oxygen, chest X-ray, immediate medical evaluation
- Associated symptoms: Chest pain, cough, wheezing, cyanosis

Severe Headache
- Description: Sudden, severe headache ("worst headache of life")
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Rule out subarachnoid hemorrhage
- Action: CT head, lumbar puncture if indicated, immediate medical evaluation
- Associated symptoms: Neck stiffness, photophobia, altered consciousness

Loss of Consciousness
- Description: Any episode of fainting or loss of consciousness
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Rule out cardiac arrhythmia, stroke, seizure
- Action: ECG, neurological exam, immediate medical evaluation
- Associated symptoms: Palpitations, chest pain, seizure activity

Severe Bleeding
- Description: Uncontrolled bleeding, especially from GI tract
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Risk of hypovolemic shock
- Action: IV access, fluid resuscitation, blood products if needed
- Associated symptoms: Dizziness, weakness, low blood pressure

Severe Allergic Reaction (Anaphylaxis)
- Description: Difficulty breathing, swelling, hives, hypotension
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Life-threatening
- Action: Epinephrine, antihistamines, steroids, immediate medical care
- Associated symptoms: Swelling of face/tongue, wheezing, rash

Stroke Symptoms
- Description: Sudden weakness, numbness, speech difficulty, vision loss
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Time-sensitive treatment window
- Action: CT head, neurological exam, consider thrombolytics
- Associated symptoms: Facial droop, arm weakness, confusion

Heart Attack Symptoms
- Description: Chest pain, pressure, or discomfort
- Urgency: IMMEDIATE - Time-sensitive treatment
- Action: ECG, cardiac enzymes, aspirin, immediate medical care
- Associated symptoms: Shortness of breath, nausea, arm/jaw pain, sweating

HIGH PRIORITY RED FLAGS:

Severe Pain
- Description: Pain rated 8-10/10, especially if sudden onset
- Urgency: HIGH - Requires immediate evaluation
- Action: Pain assessment, appropriate imaging, pain management
- Associated symptoms: Location-specific symptoms

High Fever (>39°C or 102°F)
- Description: Persistent high fever, especially in children or elderly
- Urgency: HIGH - Could indicate serious infection
- Action: Blood cultures, appropriate antibiotics, supportive care
- Associated symptoms: Chills, rigors, confusion

Severe Vomiting or Diarrhea
- Description: Persistent, leading to dehydration
- Urgency: HIGH - Risk of dehydration and electrolyte imbalance
- Action: IV fluids, electrolyte replacement, antiemetics
- Associated symptoms: Weakness, dizziness, decreased urine output

MODERATE PRIORITY RED FLAGS:

Persistent Cough with Blood
- Description: Hemoptysis (coughing up blood)
- Urgency: MODERATE-HIGH - Requires investigation
- Action: Chest X-ray, sputum culture, consider CT chest
- Associated symptoms: Chest pain, shortness of breath

Severe Dizziness or Vertigo
- Description: Persistent, affecting daily activities
- Urgency: MODERATE - Rule out serious causes
- Action: Neurological exam, consider imaging if indicated
- Associated symptoms: Nausea, hearing loss, tinnitus

GENERAL PRINCIPLES:
- When in doubt, err on the side of caution
- Red flag symptoms require immediate medical evaluation
- Do not delay treatment for red flag symptoms
- Document all red flag symptoms clearly
- Educate patients about when to seek immediate medical attention
